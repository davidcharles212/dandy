## 1. THE SYNTHESIS: 29 swings collapse into five compounding systems

The three panels, working independently, converged hard. Deduplicated, the swings form five systems:

### SYSTEM A — "THE TRUTH ENGINE" (the COA made into a universe)
The DR program built /COA as a compliance-grade proof spine. Panel consensus: it can be the entire brand's operating system.
- **The Dandy Index** (Opus #2) — invent kratom's missing unit: MIT = mitragynine mg per labeled serving, from that lot's COA, printed on every pack, computed per-lot for powder, open-licensed. Rebuilds the Figma's Tier 1–4 as composition bands and *deletes* the effect-ladder language legal already flagged.
- **De-Strained** (Opus #1) — refuse the category's unverifiable strain folklore; name by chemistry; /no-strains manifesto. The Index's necessary twin: kill the false vocabulary, supply the true one.
- **Batch Passport** (Sonnet #1) — QR→/COA as a flight-tracker journey (Harvested → Tested → Packed → Your bag), batch-bound to the pack in hand.
- **Batch Board** (Sonnet #3) + **Numbered Batches** (Fable #5) + **Lot 001 / Single Lot** (Opus #4/#8) — one batch culture: every batch numbered, counted, posted at test time (public changelog), per-batch raccoon label art, honest drop mechanics, the founding lot sold AS the founding lot ("you'll write the first reviews — that's the deal"), and powder as the terroir line (region/vein/harvest/assayed %) vs gummies as the precision line.
- **The Reject Shelf** (Opus #5) — publish the batches Dandy refused to sell. "A test you always pass is indistinguishable from a test you never ran." Launch the shelf empty with its policy; the first entry is the news.
- **Mitragynine Museum / COA literacy** (Sonnet #6) + **Open Lab Desk** (Fable #8) — teach anyone to read any COA, then referee the category's paper (anonymized version first: "we read 10 Amazon kratom COAs — here's the pattern").

### SYSTEM B — "THE RECEIPTS" (honesty as the named brand platform)
- **The Receipts platform** (Fable #2) + **The Receipt cost breakdown** (Opus #6) — one page: every COA, the itemized pouch cost (with testing as the line "we'd never cut," and the high-risk-processing line only this category can use), the dated Refusal List (no 7-OH, no effect ladders, no fake reviews, no countdowns, no exit popups), and an append-only "what we got wrong" changelog.
- **The Line** (Fable #3) — the published, versioned, self-evidenced standard for legitimate consumer kratom, timed to the 2025 FDA/state moves against 7-OH isolates. **Fable's highest-conviction single bet**: the category is visibly splitting into gas-station-synthetic vs botanical leaf, and nobody has named the split for consumers.
- **The Ceiling** (Opus #3) — a published, code-enforced cap on how much Dandy will sell one customer per 30 days. The costliest and therefore most credible honesty signal available; a documented policy predating regulatory pressure.
- **Open Books** (Fable #10) — the monthly build-in-public dispatch ("what it costs to launch a brand nobody will let you advertise"). Solo-builder stage is the asset; incumbents can't start telling the truth in month 40.

### SYSTEM C — "START LOW, DESIGNED" (the compliance mandate as product experience)
- **First Bag Ritual** (Sonnet #4) — Headspace-paced 4-screen first-use walkthrough off the pack QR; start-low as onboarding *pacing*, label-verbatim content.
- **Strength Compass** (Sonnet #2) — the preference-translator (experience × desired intensity → Tier, reasoning shown, zero capture/inference). Deliberately argues past the quiz no-build: that ruling scored lead-gen quizzes; this is an REI gear-finder.
- **Serving Journal** (Sonnet #5) — print-first, never computes or suggests; the restraint IS the compliance feature.
- **Find Your Dandy leveled onboarding** (Sonnet #7) — orders as levels, escalation 100% user-declared, never inferred. Highest compliance risk in System C; the guardrail is the design spec.
- **The Flight** (Opus #10) — discovery sampler, v1 assembled from existing 10-ct SKUs + log card: the only offer where START LOW and commercial interest point the same direction.

### SYSTEM D — "THE CHANNEL WHERE ADS CAN'T GO" (WOM + education + objects)
- **The Skeptic's Page** (Fable #1) — /learn/for-the-worried, written to the buyer's worried partner/parent; arms the advocate against the objector; owns the worried-searcher queries. **Cheapest high-conviction swing in the entire pass — ships this week.**
- **The Explainer Seat** (Fable #4) — the Hoffmann play: a real editorial masthead ("The Leaf Report by Dandy"), useful to people who never buy; in an ad-banned category the educator seat IS the acquisition seat, and it is empty.
- **The 50-State Ledger** (Fable #7) — the definitive neutral kratom-legality record; converts the mandated restricted-state page into an acquisition engine.
- **Name the Occasion** (Fable #9) — the Seedlip vocabulary play ("the third-drink hour"); occasion = *when*, not *what it does* = safest benefit territory; seeded via sober-curious/NA-drinks creators.
- **The Raccoon** (Fable #6) — the Liquid Death-grade mascot of the misunderstood (stigmatized, intelligent, washes everything = the lab joke that draws itself); merch people will actually wear; barred by written rule from effect territory.
- **The Gear** (Opus #9) — functional objects as media: the precision scale answering the powder fear, QR on everything.
- **Two-Gears Compass** (Sonnet #9) — the shareable no-capture micro-tool; cheap build, honestly-ranked-speculative payoff.
- **Order, Tracked Honestly** (Sonnet #8) — flight-tracker order status with batch binding; never renders an unverified milestone.

### SYSTEM E — "THE PASS" (the business-model hedge that's also a better product)
- **The Pass** (Opus #7) — prepaid 3/6 pouches, one charge, **no card on file**, ship-on-demand, never expires. Delivers every subscription economic with zero recurring-billing dependency — turning owner decision #2 (the processor risk) into a superior offer, and resolving the two-axis tension: the cheapest option and the committed option finally align. v1 is concierge (first 25–50 fulfilled by hand — honest; fake automation is not). Gates: stored-value/escheatment law + deferred-revenue accounting.

## 3. REQUIRED NEW RULING (blocks the batch mechanics until made)

The DR guardrails ban stock meters outright (F-11/F-12) — correctly, because they ban *theater*. Real finite lots look identical on screen. Adopt Opus's machine-enforceable clause into §3.3#9 of the DR proposal and the release scan:

> A scarcity display may render only when (a) the number is server-authoritative Shopify inventory of a lot flagged finite, (b) the lot's total size was published before the lot went on sale, (c) the counter never resets or increases, and (d) sold-out resolves to a permanent archive page, never a restock promise. Any count failing any clause fails the release scan.

Without this ruling, Lot 001, Numbered Batches, and Single Lot are **blocked, not exceptions**.

The swings aren't decorations on the DR program; they're the DR program's honesty mandate scaled from a page rule into a company.
